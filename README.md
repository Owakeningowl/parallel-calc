# Parallel Emerald Calc

A copy of Joggel's Pokémon Parallel Emerald damage calculator
(https://joggel19.github.io/parallel-emerald-calc/index.html?gen=8) with:

- every Against the Odds trainer built in (the trainer doc's "Against the Odds & Hard without EVs" import), so any
  trainer's Pokémon can be picked in Pokémon 2 without importing anything;
- a dark mode toggle in the header;
- Null-style sprite panels: Team / Box / Trash under Pokémon 1 (your imported sets) and the Opposing Team with
  Previous / Next Trainer under Pokémon 2.

Live: https://owakeningowl.github.io/parallel-calc/

Trainer levels assume your highest-level Pokémon is 50, as in the import: adjust them to your own level.
